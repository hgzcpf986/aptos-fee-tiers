## What people actually mean when they search "buy Aptos"

The intent behind the keyword splits into a few different jobs, and the right answer depends on which one you have:

## APT in one minute

## Market order or limit order?

At VIP 0 the maker and taker rates are identical, so the fee argument for limit orders doesn't kick in until higher tiers, where maker rates run below taker rates — 0.090% versus 0.095% at VIP 5, for example. What a limit order does for you at every tier is control price. A market order takes the best available offer and keeps walking the book if your size is bigger than the top level, which quietly costs more on thin pairs.

On APT/USDT, liquidity is deep enough that the difference is small. If you're buying a size that would visibly move a thin altcoin pair, use a limit order and let it work. If you're buying a few hundred dollars and want it done, market is fine.

One more option worth knowing: Gate has offered APT/BTC and APT/ETH pairs alongside the USDT market since the token listed, so if your balance is sitting in BTC or ETH you don't have to sell into stablecoins first.

Gate also runs an APT/USDT perpetual contract. If your goal is to own APT, spot is the instrument. Perpetuals are a different product wearing the same ticker.

## New-user rewards: what's real, what has strings

Gate advertises a welcome reward of up to $10,000 for new accounts. Read the terms before you treat that as cash:

- **Rewards arrive as vouchers, not withdrawable balances.** Token vouchers have to be activated within a set window after crediting — Gate's own referral terms mention a 14-day activation period — and expire if you don't claim them.
- **The package is task-based.** Typical tasks: complete identity verification, deposit a minimum amount and keep it in the account for at least two hours, then place a qualifying trade.
- **Not every deposit counts.** Per Gate's posted rules, valid deposits include on-chain deposits, fiat deposits, P2P purchases and card or third-party fiat buys. GateCode transfers, gift cards and debit/credit card deposits routed through certain channels are excluded. Stablecoin pairs also don't count toward trading volume requirements.
- **Referral rewards don't stack.** Gate states these rewards can't be combined with referral commissions or other referral campaigns.
- **The referral program excludes specific regions.** Gate's referral terms list Belgium, the United Kingdom, France, Germany, the Netherlands, Turkey, Austria and South Korea as ineligible. Separately, Gate's user agreement restricts overall service for residents of some jurisdictions, naming the US, Canada, Iran and Cuba among them.

That's the honest read: the headline number is a ceiling across multiple stages, most of it tied to trading volume you'd have to generate anyway. The useful part for a straightforward APT purchase is the smaller welcome tasks.

## Things the standard "how to buy" pages skip

**Custody is a choice you make by default.** Leaving APT on an exchange is convenient and keeps you exposed to exchange risk. Moving it to a self-custody wallet puts the keys in your hands and makes you solely responsible for them. Pick deliberately.

**Token supply matters more than the chart.** With a 2.1 billion cap and a large portion still to be distributed, APT's circulating supply grows over time. That's not a reason to avoid it, but it's a reason to look beyond price alone.

**Region rules can block you after the fact.** Verification is not just a formality; accounts opened from restricted jurisdictions can be limited regardless of how the purchase went.

**Fee schedules change.** Gate amended its spot and futures fee structure on 9 April 2026, and the top VIP tiers shifted as a result. Any fee figure you read in a blog post — including the ones above — deserves a live check before you size a large order.
